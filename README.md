### 🌟 Overview

[**HornLib (hornlib.com)**](https://hornlib.com) is a comprehensive online reference for hornists, brass students, and conservatory educators. Featuring clear vector valve diagrams for **single F horns**, **single Bb horns**, and **full F/Bb double horns** (including thumb-trigger combinations), HornLib helps musicians master alternate fingerings, stopped-horn transpositions, and harmonic intonation tendencies across the instrument's entire four-octave range.

### 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

- **Does HornLib cover double horn trigger fingerings?**  
  Yes — full F and Bb side fingerings are clearly differentiated with recommended orchestral crossover points.
- **Are warm-up routines and studies available?**  
  Yes — public-domain overtone studies, lip-slur drills, and classical etudes (such as Kopprasch Op. 6) are freely accessible online.
